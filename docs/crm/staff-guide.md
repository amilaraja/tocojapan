# TOCO CRM: staff guide

Admin → **CRM**. Everything about a buyer is in one **Case** (reference C-2026-00042).

## Your day
1. Open **My work**. It lists your deadlines (nearest first), today's follow-ups, tasks, new Cases and Cases nobody has touched for 3 days.
2. Open a Case.
   - Reply with **Follow up → Email the customer**: pick a template, check it, send.
   - Or use **Message in their account**.
   - Chatted on WhatsApp? Use **Log call / WhatsApp**. It counts as your reply.
3. Tick the **Checklist** as things happen. Many steps tick themselves:
   - a PayPal payment
   - an order placed on the website
   - the car marked reserved or sold
   - the B/L entered on the order
4. Always leave a **next follow-up date** (Status → Set next follow-up).

## The five steps
01 Search & Order → 02 Payment → 03 Car Shipment → 04 Clearing → 05 Car Received.
- A Case moves to the next step by itself when the required items are ticked.
- If something is missing, "Move to next stage" tells you what.
- Moving back needs an admin and a reason.

## Waiting for the buyer
Use **Status → Waiting on customer**. Reply deadlines pause, and they restart when the buyer writes.

## Finishing a Case
- **Won** happens by itself when the buyer clicks "Car received", or when you tick it.
- **Mark lost** needs a reason (price, bought elsewhere …).
- **Close** is for duplicates, spam, and people who are not buyers. Spam never gets emails from us.

## Your nickname
**CRM → My profile → Nickname.** Buyers only ever see this name, never your real name. Their replies still come to your own inbox.
- **Away dates** stop new Cases coming to you.
- **Morning summary only** cuts down your emails. Late-reply alerts still arrive at once.

## Bank details
Never type account numbers. Write `{bank_details}` in an email and the CRM fills in the details from Site settings.

## Admins
- **Pipeline** shows every sale by step.
- **Team overview** shows each person's speed, SLA % and results.
- **SLA monitor** lists late and at-risk Cases.
- **Reports** have CSV that opens in Excel.
- Configure in **Queues** (who gets which Cases), **Stages & steps**, **Email templates** and **Settings** (hours, holidays, deadlines, emails, reasons, notifications).
- **Give staff access** on the Users screen with the role CRM Agent, CRM Operations or CRM Admin, then add them to a Queue.
