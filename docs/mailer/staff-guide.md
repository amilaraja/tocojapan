# TOCO Mailer: staff guide

**What it does:** you pick vehicles from our stock, and TOCO Mailer builds the email and puts it into Brevo **as a draft**. Nothing is ever sent from TOCO Mailer. You test, approve and send in Brevo.

## Make a new campaign

1. In the admin, open **Mailer, Campaigns** and press **New campaign**. The quickest start is to open last week's campaign and press **Duplicate**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Internal name:** only staff see it.
   - **Subject line:** up to 120 characters.
   - **Preview text:** the grey line shown after the subject in the inbox.
   - **Small red heading**, **Headline** and **Intro paragraph:** the text above the vehicles.
   - **From:** who the email comes from.
   - **Send to these Brevo lists:** who will get it.
3. Pick a **Banner**. New banners go under **Mailer, Banners**: exactly 1200 × 440 pixels, JPG or PNG.
4. Press **Add vehicles**. Type a stock number (like E02056) or a model name, or use the filters (make, body type, price, Hot deal / New). Tick the vehicles and press **Add**.
   - A campaign needs **2 to 12 vehicles**.
   - Only vehicles for sale appear. Sold vehicles are never shown.
5. Put the vehicles in order by dragging them, or with the ↑ ↓ arrows.
6. Press **Preview** and check both **Desktop** and **Mobile**.

## Send it to Brevo

1. Press **Push to Brevo as draft**. This creates a draft in Brevo. **Nothing is sent.**
2. You will see "Draft created in Brevo. Nothing has been sent yet." Press **Open in Brevo**.
3. In Brevo, **send a test** to yourself, check it on your phone, then get approval and send.
4. Changed something after pushing? The campaign shows **Changed since push**. Press **Push to Brevo as draft** again to update the same Brevo draft.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "E02056 was sold. Remove it before pushing." | Delete that vehicle from the list (bin icon), then push again. |
| "Price changed from $2,750 to $2,650. The email will show the new price." | Nothing: the email already uses the new price. |
| "Add at least 2 vehicles before pushing." | Add more vehicles. |
| "This campaign is no longer a draft in Brevo…" | It was already sent. Press **Duplicate** to start a new one. |
| "Brevo could not be reached…" | Wait a few minutes and try again. |

## Campaign status

- **Draft:** not in Brevo yet.
- **In Brevo (draft):** pushed; waiting for someone to test and send it in Brevo.
- **Changed since push:** edited after pushing; push again.
- **Sent:** sent from Brevo. Opens and clicks appear within an hour.
- **Archived:** put away.

## Inbox importer (managers only)

New enquiries are added to Brevo automatically. Buyers who unsubscribed are never added back. See **Mailer, Importer**:
- **Run log:** each check of the inbox.
- **Contact search:** what happened to one email address.
- **Ignore list:** addresses or domains we never want added.

## Beware of fraud

Every email ends with our fraud warning. Never change our bank details in an email.
